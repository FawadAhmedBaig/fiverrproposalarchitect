Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Proposal Architect Pro, we may collect the following information:

  • Google Account Email: Used for authentication and subscription identity. Collected via Google OAuth when you sign in.
  • Subscription Status: Whether your account has an active Pro subscription, stored securely in our database.
  • Extension Usage Data: Proposal generation counts (for free-tier enforcement). Stored locally in your browser only.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your Fiverr project briefs, scraped data, or generated proposals to any server.
  • We do not collect your API keys (OpenAI/Gemini). They are stored locally in your browser's extension storage.
  • We do not collect your profile information (name, skills, experience). This is stored locally only.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: Your Google email is used to identify your account for subscription management.
  • Subscription Verification: We check your subscription status against our database when the extension loads.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google OAuth: For authentication. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Paddle.com: Payment processing. Paddle is the Merchant of Record. Subject to Paddle's Privacy Policy.
  • OpenAI / Google Gemini: AI proposal generation. Your API keys and prompts are sent directly from your browser to these services. We do not proxy or store this data.
  • Firebase: Secure database for subscription records only.

5. Data Security

All communication between the extension and our servers uses HTTPS encryption. Webhook payloads from Paddle are verified using HMAC-SHA256 signatures. Your subscription data is stored in a secured Firebase Firestore database.

6. Data Retention

We retain your subscription record (email and subscription status) for as long as your account exists. You may request deletion by contacting us.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, contact: support@proposal-architect-pro.com
