Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

All payments are processed by Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record. Refund requests are handled by Paddle's customer support team.

1. Refund Eligibility

We offer a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase if you are unsatisfied with the Pro subscription. No questions asked.

2. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

  • Locate your purchase receipt email from Paddle.
  • Click the "Manage Subscription" or "Request Refund" link in that email.
  • Alternatively, contact Paddle support directly at paddle.com/support.

3. After the 14-Day Window

After the 14-day refund window, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis at Paddle's discretion. You may still cancel your subscription at any time to prevent future charges.

4. Cancellation vs. Refund

  • Cancellation: Stops future billing. You retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
  • Refund: Returns your payment and immediately revokes Pro access.

5. Processing Time

Refunds are typically processed within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider.

6. Contact

For refund inquiries, contact: support@proposal-architect-pro.com or reach out to Paddle directly through your purchase receipt.
